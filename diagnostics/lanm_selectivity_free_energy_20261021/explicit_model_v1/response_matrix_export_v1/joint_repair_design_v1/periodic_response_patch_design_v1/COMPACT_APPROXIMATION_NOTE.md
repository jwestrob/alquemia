# Compact numerical approximation: design assessment only

A separately declared **C² numerical approximation** can simplify the trajectory implementation: let F_c(r)=w(r)F(r), with 0≤w≤1, w=1 through 20 Å and w=0 from 21 Å, and first/second derivatives matching zero at both switch endpoints. In an orthorhombic cell with shortest side strictly greater than 42 Å, at most one image of each water can contribute. Evaluating F_c on the nearest image therefore equals its complete periodic lattice sum; this is an exact statement about the **compact approximate Hamiltonian**, not an identification of minimum image with the original central+26 or infinite noncompact sum. Nearest-image branch changes occur outside support, where F_c and its first two derivatives vanish. Thus a consistent h/H construction, shared by both mutual right-hand sides, can have conservative coordinate and cell derivatives without maintaining 27 physical-image identities. All environment-chain derivatives, the switch derivative w′F, metal reaction forces, and active-image strain virials remain necessary; native electrostatic/PME terms retain their own periodic definitions.

The error budget must be declared before adopting this approximation. It must bound both the removed/modified nearest-image contribution beyond 20 Å and the entire nonnearest-image lattice sum, whose distances are at least half the shortest cell side for orthogonal nearest representatives. A small value of F(20 Å), or the previous saved-box 27-image remainder alone, is insufficient. Use positive population/lattice bounds with the fixed water count and declared cell domain, and propagate δF, δF′ and any required δF″; transition errors contain w′F and w″F+2w′F′ as well as the original tail derivatives. The existing environment Lipschitz/Hessian bounds can then bound changes in h/H and their explicit derivatives, including changes to retained waters' collective factors. A uniform trajectory certificate needs fixed coefficient/alpha/damping bounds, the population, and an enforced orthorhombic cell domain maintaining the support margin; a changing box cannot silently cross that domain. Bounds on the total self-consistent energy/force error additionally require response-inverse stability and the relevant induced-dipole/native derivative bounds. Numerical solver and PME application error remain separate. This is a plausible simpler trajectory route if its prospective budget passes, with a declared approximation error rather than a new fitted physical cutoff. No bound was evaluated, switch selected, native source changed, or sampling authorized here. The frozen [static-image plan](PLAN.md) and its dependencies remain unchanged; the admitted saved geometry tail fact does not itself qualify this alternative.
