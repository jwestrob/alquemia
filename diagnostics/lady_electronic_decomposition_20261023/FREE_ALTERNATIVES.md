# Free implementation candidates for the fixed La/Dy decomposition

2026-10-23. Documentation/source review only; no installation, build or calculation. This records findings so they are not lost. It does not authorize a different Hamiltonian or decomposition campaign.

## CP2K: native ALMO, exact Dy55 reader repair under review

CP2K provides native molecular-fragment initialization, block-diagonal ALMO optimization and unrestricted-domain `FULL_SCF`. For a genuinely nonperiodic system its native fragment/frozen/block/full absolute energies support the requested closure, without interpreting a charged periodic sublattice. The current stock ECP reader rejects core count 55; changing Dy55 to Dy54/Dy60 is unacceptable. Root is considering an isolated, source-reviewed bookkeeping repair, not altered ECP coefficients. See `CP2K_PILOT_REVIEW.md` and `CP2K_COMPATIBILITY.md`. [CP2K ALMO controls](https://manual.cp2k.org/trunk/CP2K_INPUT/FORCE_EVAL/DFT/ALMO_SCF.html), [inspected ECP reader](https://github.com/cp2k/cp2k/blob/62906da87cd0e92000c341d4500e1ed4a5a6af9c/src/atom_types.F)

## Qbics: promising documented TSO/BLW candidate, not qualified

- The official manual names `eda type tso` as block-localized-wavefunction EDA and reports frozen/Heitler–London contributions, polarization, CT and total interaction energy. `gks` gives a different decomposition and must not substitute. BSSE correction is on by default; its CT correction must not be mixed with a raw total. An atomic-fragment initialization option is already native. EDA requires `scf type U`, so effective singlet multiplicity alone does not establish equality with the archived restricted state; alpha/beta equality and full-energy agreement require verification. [EDA controls](https://qbics.info/doc/keywords/eda.html)
- A native charged-fragment example explicitly compares a TSO constrained total energy with the ordinary delocalized total and identifies their difference as uncorrected CT. This supports the required counterfactual rather than a population-only proxy. The `frag num_electrons` syntax heading conflicts with examples and explanatory text that clearly use fragment **charges**; source/parser verification is needed before input construction. [Constrained-state example and fragment syntax](https://qbics.info/doc/keywords/scfguess.html)
- PBE0 is explicitly listed. Custom Gaussian94-format semilocal ECPs and basis definitions are documented. Particularly relevant is the supplied Ce(H2O)8(3+) example with an odd 47-electron frozen-f core, g local channel and effective singlet. This is stronger evidence than generic ECP availability, but does not prove the exact archived Dy55 path works. Preserve every radial exponent/power/coefficient and orbital contraction; verify parser/integral conventions rather than trusting similar names. [PBE0](https://qbics.info/doc/keywords/scf.html), [custom ECP and Ce example](https://qbics.info/doc/keywords/pseudopotential.html), [custom bases](https://qbics.info/doc/keywords/basis.html)
- The documented `grimmedisp` correction is **D3**, not D4. Disable it for this comparison. Any use of archived geometry-only D4 must retain a separate exact ledger, added once, with fragment/reference bookkeeping specified. Do not label D3 or bare PBE0 as the archived PBE0-D4 total. [Dispersion controls](https://qbics.info/doc/keywords/grimmedisp.html)

The official site offers source and Linux executables, including a listed 2.0 release dated 2026-04-01, through a registration form. This review did not register, download or inspect its license; access conditions, installed version and runtime compatibility remain unverified. [Official download page](https://www.qbics.info/home/qbics-download.html)

**Decision:** Qbics is worth retaining as a documented native BLW/TSO option if needed. Call it TSO/BLW explicitly; do not silently relabel it as a particular Q-Chem ALMO definition. Its frozen reference, exact ECP implementation and effective state require source/runtime verification before it could satisfy the requested pilot. No further Qbics investigation or alternative-method construction is active in this review.
