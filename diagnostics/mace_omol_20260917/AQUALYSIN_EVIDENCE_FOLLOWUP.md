# Aqualysin follow-up: retain the site-mapping uncertainty

Read-only follow-up2026-09-17. No new labels, coordinates or scores.

The2002primary NMR paper is accessible using the J-STAGE PDF path with
`/-char/en`. Its Table1 confirms association constants6200M^-1(Ca) and
1100M^-1(La) for the weak site at22C,pH6, with the strong Ca site occupied.
The second La-binding site remains distinct. This verifies the existing
curation; it does not identify a4DZTatom selector.
[Primary paper](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/bbb/66/6/66_6_1281/_pdf/-char/en).

A2019VPR study discusses the proposed weak aqualysin site as Ca-1 based on
structural analogy and effects on stability. That is an inference, not a direct
site-resolved assignment of the2002La/Ca assay. Naming differs between papers,
so a bare Ca1/Ca3 label is insufficient. Preserve the mapping uncertainty.
[Primary study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1570963918302085),
[author repository containing the paper](https://opinvisindi.is/server/api/core/bitstreams/60f398cb-139f-4797-851a-899a5d0245c9/content).

The4DZTsource also has two Ca ions and an inhibitor, beyond the current
single-site prepared-input interface's scope. Do not remove the other Ca or
inhibitor silently, select whichever site agrees with the assay, or import PQQ
bands. A future test could retain an ordered vector over all plausible sites,
with the ambiguity explicit and a prespecified interpretation, after physical
preparation and a suitable affinity comparison are established.
[Deposited structure](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4DZT).
