## Method (production)

**One-line definition:** ΔΔE = (E[Ca-cluster] − E[La-cluster]) − DIFF_AQUO, where DIFF_AQUO = E[Ca·6H₂O] − E[La·6H₂O] = −646.064555551745 Ha at r²SCAN-3c/CPCM(Water).

Pipeline (auto-handled by `scripts/process_inbox.sh`):

1. **Drop CIF in `inbox/`** with La placed in the candidate pocket.
2. **PQQ-aware routing** (2026-05-11): if the CIF contains a PQQ-like ligand (≥12C + ≥6O + ≥1N in a single `LIG_*` residue), route through `normalize_af3_cif.py` → `protonate_cif.py` → `carve_with_pqq.py` (PQQ included in QM region). Otherwise: `protonate_cif.py` → `carve_generic.py` (protein-only).
3. **Carve QM cluster** at r_first_shell = **3.0 Å** (tightened from 3.2 Å on 2026-05-11). Sidechain dict: ASP, GLU, ASN, GLN, SER, **TYR** (added 2026-05-11). Backbone-O carving (added 2026-05-11) — residues with backbone C=O within 3.0 Å of metal get C+O added with link-H caps.
4. **Run 4 ORCA SPs** at r²SCAN-3c CPCM(Water) DefGrid3 NoAutostart: La form, Ca form (vertical metal swap), apo (metal removed), bulk H₂O reference.
5. **Aggregate** via `update_results_jsonl.py` — walks `*_qm/` dirs, parses energies, computes ΔΔE, classifies, appends to `results/all_results.jsonl`, regenerates `discriminator_panel_LATEST.tsv`, and auto-rebuilds `ln_class_hits.tsv` with spicy_lams annotations.

**Class thresholds** (locked by validation):
- ΔΔE ≥ +20 → **Ln-evolved** (XoxF-tier; architectural Ln preference)
- +5 to +20 → **Ln-preferring** (intrinsic carboxylate-cluster preference)
- 0 to +5 → marginal
- −5 to 0 → ambiguous
- < −5 → **Ca-evolved** (MxaF-tier)
- \|ΔΔE\| > 30 → **OUTLIER** (typically Mn²⁺/Zn²⁺ small-acidic-pocket signature; biology signal, not numerical error)

## Anomaly patterns

1. **OUTLIER (−30 to −80 kcal/mol)** — Mn²⁺/Zn²⁺ pocket signature. Geometric. Treat as biology.
2. **SEVERE asymmetric carve (−100 to −400 kcal/mol)** — ΔE(metal) ~0.18–0.65 Ha off the canonical −646.06. Recurring on a subset of acidobacterium and eukaryotic candidates. Different ligand sets between Ca and La carves (typically PQQ inclusion mismatch or SCF localization difference). Flagged for manual investigation.
3. **EMPTY CARVE** (E[La] = −31.158775581143 bit-identical, 1 atom) — TYR-OH or backbone-O coordination missed by old carve dict. Fixed 2026-05-11. Recarve queue at `results/recarve_queue.tsv` processed by `scripts/process_recarve_queue.py`. 19 historical entries reprocessed; new empty-carves still arriving means the upstream fold-daemon's carve invocation may need patching too.

## Cluster discipline notes

- Memory partition: 64-core nodes; full-node `--exclusive`; OpenMP threading (`OMP_NUM_THREADS=$SLURM_CPUS_ON_NODE`); NEVER MPI/PMIX.
- ORCA 6.1.1: `/home/jwestrob/jwestrob/bin/ORCA/orca_6_1_1_linux_x86-64_shared_openmpi418_nodmrg/`
- Always use `! NoAutostart` for new SPs in dirs with prior runs.
- For Protenix CIFs: protonate with `/home/jwestrob/miniconda3/envs/fep` (pdbfixer); `lanm_qmmm` env for carve/aggregation work.
- Standard 4 SPs: La (cluster charge varies, mult 1), Ca (charge -1 vs La, mult 1), apo (charge varies, mult 1), bulk H₂O (charge 0, mult 1). Each ~5–15 min on a 64-thread node; full panel per candidate ~20–60 min wallclock.
