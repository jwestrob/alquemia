Model-choice clarification for root synthesis

What is independently supported?
The robust result is a coupled mechanical/electrical response defect, including
an incorrect La donor rotational slope across PBE0 and canonical CC. No specific
angular two-body term, anisotropic polarizability, or charge-transfer mechanism
has been isolated. The existing rotation changes both metal–O distances; even
two radial metal–oxygen interactions can produce a torque. One uniform-field
projection likewise cannot identify a local tensor. Therefore the directional
h/H architecture in REPORT.txt is a concrete modeling hypothesis, not a proved
minimum Hamiltonian or a reason to reject every scalar two-body law.

Relation to an explicit short-range pair-plus-three-body energy
That architecture is compatible with this donor evidence and may be the clearer
unified model choice. Its electrical response must be part of the same energy,
through variational dipole/charge coordinates or equivalent field couplings.
Adding only field-independent pair/triplet energies can repair energy and force
at a fixed geometry but cannot change its dipole or susceptibility. The h/H
language is one linear/quadratic representation of those electrical couplings,
not an alternative to an energy-based description. Donor evidence does not yet
determine whether a new triplet energy or a coordination-dependent two-body
electrical response supplies the needed environment dependence. The water-sector
pair/collective evidence should decide the required body order jointly.

Smallest explicit angular candidate, if root chooses that route
Use one directed donor-frame scalar c_i = n_CO,i dot rhat_MO,i, with n_CO,i set
by the actual carbon–oxygen bond, and a smooth radial envelope. Sum the same
functional form over both equivalent carboxylate oxygens; do not choose a
nearest oxygen. This introduces a defined orientation variable rather than
independent corrections for each fixture. Couple this variable to the energy
and electrical response through the same variational functional. For example,
an existing radial drive plus one donor-frame drive direction and one uniaxial
inverse-response correction is a restricted hypothesis; a free full tensor or
separate fitted scalar for each observed slope is not supported by these data.
Neither the preferred angular polynomial nor its radial/coordination dependence
is empirically identified here. Adding this variable should be justified as a
structural choice, not advertised as a measured quantum decomposition.

What can constrain the choice now, without another reference campaign?
1. Exact selected17621 dry centers, both radial steps and both rotation steps,
   complete gradients, La/Dy and paired work; canonical La rotational values
   provide an independent method check on the sign, not a replacement target.
2. Ten precise common-origin PBE0/model moments and their saved rotation changes;
   the model field calculation uses the half-dual conjugate moment. Qualified
   La susceptibility supplies one additional response projection. Retain failed
   Dy values descriptively and do not use them as admitted curvature targets.
3. Matched dry/wet central and radial energy/force fixtures, retaining individual
   metals as well as contrasts; these expose cancellation and environment
   dependence. The central hydrated-amide translation has its own small-step
   numerical qualification.
4. Already qualified donor .1 drive/stiffness/combined probe values and their
   mixed finite effects. These are valuable constraints against an architecture
   that merely repeats their radial/rotation tradeoff; they are not a new fit.

Missing coverage that cannot be claimed away
No fixed-metal–O-distance angular series, full susceptibility tensor, qualified
Dy/paired susceptibility, canonical Dy rotational derivative, general mixed-
donor many-body reference decomposition, or independent protein/environment
transfer validation is supplied. Some complete force arrays lack direction-by-
direction reference qualification. Existing finite-basis/frozen-f/state and
SCF/final-grid limits remain. A model change can be decided now; these gaps
limit what it could be claimed to explain or validate afterward.

No new extraction, numerical replay, fit, controls or molecular calls were made.
