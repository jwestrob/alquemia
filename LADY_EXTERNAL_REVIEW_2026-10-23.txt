USER'S REQUEST AND CURRENT HOLD
------------------------------
The user wants a realistic account of La versus Dy binding, and has repeatedly
asked for the root physical/modeling problem to be addressed. After roughly a
week of many small calculations and incremental parameter fits, there is still
no validated exchange free energy. The user considers the work misdirected and
has requested independent review before further scientific direction is chosen.
New scientific campaigns are paused. Preparing and publishing this handoff is
authorized; none of the suggestions below is a newly approved research plan.

WHAT THE ACTUAL SCIENTIFIC GOAL IS
--------------------------------
For a specified peptide/protein and solution condition, calculate

  DeltaG_exchange = DeltaG_bound(La -> Dy) - DeltaG_water(La -> Dy).

Negative values favor Dy. Both ions are trivalent. Static cluster energies,
geometry relaxations, and a successful numerical polarization solve are not
this free energy. The intended eventual calculation includes explicit solvent,
protein response, relevant chemical states, sampling, and uncertainty.

The initial experimental target is LBT3, with an amidated construct
NH3+–FIDTNNDGWIEGDELLA–CONH2, declared peptide charge -4 and complex charge -1.
Historical free-carboxylate LBT3 is a separate sensitivity. A 2020 ITC comparison
provides La/Dy free energies at 283.15 K and pH 6 but unresolved terminal
chemistry; a 2025 assay specifies amidation but has incomplete temperature and
numerical uncertainty information. These assays must not be silently merged.
LBT/Hans/Mex observations already inspected are development evidence, not blind
validation. The planned 0.5 kcal/mol sampling-confidence half-width is not a
demonstrated model error or an accuracy tolerance for each cluster interaction.

HOW WE ARRIVED HERE
-------------------
1. Earlier MACE/DFT comparisons and limited accommodation calculations produced
   conditional energies rather than equilibrium selectivity. A late peptide
   relaxation was unfavorable in both MACE and PBE0 vacuum energies while a
   saved fixed-force-field-charge ALPB term made the composite favorable. Agreement after
   adding the same solvent term did not independently validate that term.
2. Work moved to an explicit polarizable model based on AMOEBA/Tinker, with
   matched La/Dy water and donor reference chemistry. It accumulated gas-phase
   pair, hydrated-donor, water-removal, coordination, force, field-response and
   fragment-decomposition data, plus numerical engine work. There is no
   qualified production sampling model from this effort.
3. Repeated water/donor parameter fits and then custom electrical-response
   corrections improved selected objectives but failed other physical tests.
   The agent continued optimizing longer than the failures justified.
4. A recent radial14 candidate improved its fitting objective while all eight
   inspected individual trimer energy and dipole errors worsened; all four
   La/Dy trimer energy contrasts had the wrong sign. This prompted the new
   short-range model tested below. The new test also failed.

PBE0-D4/def2-TZVP differs from the pinned MACE-OMOL omegaB97M-V/def2-TZVPD
training Hamiltonian and lanthanide-spin treatment. Their differences cannot
uniquely be attributed to machine-learning approximation error. Choosing the
AMOEBA-based research route was not an experimentally validated replacement.

The baseline in the newest comparison is the exact archived, already developed
polarizable model, not a claim about unmodified stock AMOEBA. Its native
operator and parameters are pinned in the source/input manifests. The radial14
candidate is not silently stacked underneath the new correction.

REFERENCE DATA AND WHAT THEY DO NOT ESTABLISH
-------------------------------------------
The recent water targets are electronic PBE0 fragment energies in a common
parent orbital/auxiliary basis, with ghost functions and matched fragment
cycles. D4 is excluded from those electronic decomposition targets. La and Dy
use the recorded frozen-core/f-in-core effective-core-potential treatment, not
an established all-electron correlated ground truth. Common-basis subtraction
controls a comparison convention; it does not establish basis or functional
accuracy for the physical system.

The retained core treatment uses La ECP46 and Dy ECP55 with effective singlets;
the physical parent multiplicities are La singlet and Dy sextet. Spin-orbit
coupling is absent. An all-electron scalar-ZORA comparison changed core
treatment, basis and Dy state together, and changed a La-water radial slope
from about -4.17 to +1.55 kcal/mol/A. It establishes reference sensitivity,
not an isolated f-electron effect or a uniquely correct reference treatment.

The La dry-acetate mechanical targets retain their original PBE0-D4 protocol.
Canonical correlated calculations independently support the large La rotational
slope discrepancy, but do not validate every PBE0 electrical-response target.
Canonical Dy rotation attempts failed. Failed Dy donor susceptibility checks
are excluded, not replaced by guessed values. Tightened SCF stopping checks and
local correlated-reference controls exist, but are not a universal reference
uncertainty estimate.

Available fields principally qualify selected directional susceptibilities,
not complete polarizability tensors. Full static dipole vectors are available;
not every component has independent field-derivative qualification. The donor
is charged: its dipole depends on origin. An explicit audit found that the
compared donor coordinates, origin, units, and charge agree. The quantum dipole
was translated from center of mass to the metal-at-origin frame with the net
charge term. The large donor mismatch is not explained by that convention.

THE NEW MODEL THAT WAS ACTUALLY TESTED
------------------------------------
This was a deliberately finite effective correction, not a full electronic
structure model or a trained general-purpose machine-learned potential.

Let A0 be the cached native polarization operator, Ed and Ep the two native
driving-field conventions, U_other the baseline energy excluding polarization,
and mu_perm the baseline permanent moment. These are fixed at each saved
geometry. Added terms are a scalar mechanical energy V, a driving vector h,
and a symmetric operator correction H:

  G = A0 + H
  d = Ed + h + B F;  p = Ep + h + B F
  u = G^-1 d;        v = G^-1 p
  E = U_other + V - K mu_perm dot F - (K/2) d^T G^-1 p
  mu_conjugate = mu_perm + B^T(u+v)/2
  chi_native = B^T G^-1 B.

B applies a uniform field to all induced-dipole sites. Ed and Ep need not be
equal. This is a two-channel stationary cross functional, not a single induced
dipole minimizing a positive quadratic energy. The native physical-u dipole
and the energy-conjugate average-u/v dipole must not be conflated. K and the
native-to-atomic-unit conversion are explicit in KERNEL.py and the collector.

Crucial restriction: all new h/H entries act ONLY on donor oxygen response
components. The metal and other atoms retain their baseline self response.
No new charge-transfer variables or permanent-multipole redistribution were
implemented. The whole baseline already has many-body polarization; describing
it as pairwise or angle-free would be false.

The corrections are assigned to intact metal–ligand pairs and metal–two-ligand
triples before solving the coupled response. Acetate's two oxygens are ONE
ligand; no artificial intra-acetate three-body fragment is introduced. The
coupled solution can generate higher-order effects even from pair/triple
operator corrections. Actual fragment cycles, not isolated V coefficients,
are compared to quantum pair/trimer targets.

Implemented geometry basis:
  w(r) = max(1-r^2/6^2,0)^3, with distances in Angstrom;
  n = metal-to-O vector / sqrt(r^2+0.1^2);
  b = a softened local covalent ligand-frame direction;
  rho = (r^2-2.5^2)/2.5^2.
Pair energy uses w(v0 + vr*rho + va*n dot b). Pair h uses radial/frame vectors;
pair H uses their outer products. Triples use products of contact weights,
inter-ligand angles, cross-directed fields and symmetric cross-O response.
Smoothness/symmetry/separation properties do not establish that this basis is
physically sufficient. The particular limited basis was an agent choice.

TWO FITTING CAMPAIGNS, NOT TWO NEW QUANTUM EXPERIMENTS
--------------------------------------------------
Both reused 64 saved operator geometries: 54 water states, two dry-donor
centers, and eight La donor displacement endpoints. No new quantum calculations
were performed for these fits. Each ran on a 24-CPU standard_expand allocation
with 16 workers. They ran consecutively, not simultaneously.

shared12:
  water pair: v0, hf, krr, kff;
  water–water triple: v0, va, h_neighbor, k_cross;
  acetate pair: vr, va, hf, kff.
All coefficients are shared across metals; all other kernel amplitudes are zero.

metal15:
  Split only the water pair v0 and triple v0/va into separate La/Dy values.
  Electrical h/H and acetate coefficients remain shared.
Although the generic kernel supports water-frame angular energy terms, the
actual campaigns set water pair va and triple vf to zero. Their direct water
mechanics therefore lacks hydrogen-frame orientation dependence at fixed O.

The second version tests the specific restriction that identical La/Dy
geometries with shared mechanical coefficients have identical added V energies.
It does not introduce metal-specific electrical response.

Targets in the objective:
  - Five susceptibility projections: La/Dy water dimer and far trimer, plus
    the qualified La dry-acetate direction.
  - 34 pair-energy cycles and signed pair moments.
  - Four W9 near/far trimer energy and signed moment cycles.
  - La donor full static dipole and two half-step mechanical slopes.

Omissions from fitting:
  - Complete W8/W9 shell energies and their pair/collective decomposition.
  - W8 trimer targets, full-step donor slopes and the Dy donor moment.
  - Wet donors, mixed water/donor environments and amide coefficients.
  - Full water coordinate forces and full electrical-response tensors.
Some omitted quantities were inspected previously and/or reported afterward;
they are consumed checks, not a genuinely blind holdout set.

Loss = equal mean of five squared-error families: chi, mu, pair E, W9 trimer E,
and donor slope. The mu family itself averages pair, trimer and donor vector
MSEs. Scales of 1 a0^3, 1 Debye, 1 kcal/mol and 1 kcal/mol/Angstrom are objective
weights, NOT scientific accuracy gates. Thus a small aggregate score cannot
override a wrong individual observable.

Bounds: V coefficients +/-100 kcal/mol, h +/-0.1 e/Angstrom^2, H +/-1 Angstrom^-3.
All 64 states require the baseline-normalized total operator's minimum
eigenvalue to be at least 1e-6. Each campaign used one zero and 15 fixed Sobol
H starts, conditional H then linear h/V solves, followed by one full joint
SLSQP optimization per start (100 iterations, ftol 1e-10). The starts collapsed
to essentially the same solutions. They are NOT evidence of 16 independent
basins or global optimality. Raw outcomes and coefficients are retained.

POST-FIT REPRESENTABILITY CHECK: A CONDITIONAL MATHEMATICAL RESULT
----------------------------------------------------------------
A saved-operator calculation asked whether larger coefficients could fix the
electrical failures without changing where the corrections act. It did not run
another fit or quantum calculation.

For A0=L L^T, b=B times the measured field direction, and U spanning all allowed
stiffness-update directions, an optimistic susceptibility floor is

  ||(I-P_span(L^-1 U)) L^-1 b||^2,

with the native-to-source unit conversion applied. It allows unlimited positive
stiffness on U and is also a lower bound for any stable symmetric update confined
to that support. A separate calculation expands U to ALL Cartesian components
of all donor oxygens, avoiding dependence on near-collinear local-frame vectors.

                          full-O floor     reference, a0^3
  La water dimer            16.73806          15.47168
  La far trimer             22.21808          22.01653
  Dy water dimer            10.65143          13.15803
  Dy far trimer             16.33707          19.88113
  La dry acetate            35.92750          47.88102

Only the two La water targets fall below those floors. For the dimer, all four
saved energy/moment and full/half-step reference estimates lie between
15.471680 and 15.481986. Their numerical discrepancies are much smaller than
the approximately 1.26 a0^3 gap. This is NOT a bound on PBE0/ECP/basis error.

For fixed A0, permanent moments and field coupling B, oxygen-only h/H cannot
reproduce those retained La-water targets, even with unrestricted coefficients.
h and V cannot change fixed-geometry susceptibility. This does not establish
which atom or microscopic mechanism is physically wrong. Changing only O/non-O
off-diagonal coupling while keeping the non-O self block fixed also leaves an
O-clamped lower bound. Some non-O self response, field coupling, or the broader
response law must change to match these targets under the stated assumptions.

For the static dipole, m=(u+v)/2 obeys A0(m-m0)=h-Hm. Therefore the possible
moment change is confined to range(B^T A0^-1 U_mu), where U_mu spans H and h.
The present donor frame basis leaves a 2.14064-Debye target component outside
that range. Full Cartesian oxygen response removes this particular obstruction.
It therefore does NOT prove that donor response must extend beyond oxygen.
These are ordinary-double calculations with reported singular values/ranks,
not interval certificates or proofs about every possible polarizable model.

WHAT IS ESTABLISHED, AND WHAT IS STILL SPECULATION
------------------------------------------------
Established within this dataset/model comparison:
  - The finite new candidate is not an acceptable interaction model.
  - Independent short-range energy terms can fit selected mechanical targets,
    but did not solve electrical response or complete-shell transfer.
  - The oxygen-only response restriction cannot match the retained La-water
    susceptibility targets with the rest of the baseline fixed.
  - No validated solvated protein/water exchange prediction exists.

Not established:
  - That charge transfer, Pauli repulsion, nonlinearity, or explicit four-body
    interactions are the unique missing mechanism.
  - That DFT is sufficiently accurate for every target, or that another MACE
    checkpoint would solve the problem.
  - That simply fitting the parent energies too would yield a transferable
    Hamiltonian, or that more optimizer starts would resolve these failures.
  - That the proposed successor below would work.

Untested ideas raised by the agents include metal-centered/environment-dependent
self response, broader ligand-frame response, consistent permanent-multipole
redistribution, and a collective shell energy with correct lower-fragment
limits. These are competing possibilities, not conclusions or implementations.
The current data do not uniquely identify all these freedoms. Adding them all
could merely create a more flexible interpolator.
