DECISION

Change the field-coupled donor response law, retaining independent equilibrium
drive and response stiffness. The next structural degree of freedom should
separate radial response from donor-frame directional response. Repeating the
shared radius/damping fit, globally softening polarization, adding only a
field-independent energy term, or repeating the already tested scalar donor
h/H amplitudes is not supported as a sufficient repair.

A concrete architecture is the existing conservative dual functional with a
donor-local driving field h_i and an orientation-resolved oxygen response block:
  G = alpha^-1 - T + H(R)
  G u = E_d + h;  G v = E_p + h.
Retain the existing isotropic H component and add a distinct directional block,
for example beta_aniso*(n_i n_i^T-I/3), with n_i defined continuously from the
donor's molecular frame. Give h_i an independently controlled donor-frame
direction in addition to the existing radial metal–O drive. This introduces a
way to change electrical moment/torque without forcing the same radial change.
Radial and local coordination dependence must be explicit and smooth if the
same law is to describe dry and hydrated fixtures. Equivalent carboxylate oxygens
must remain symmetry-related. The total energy must generate forces, frame
torques and field derivatives consistently, with the actual stability gate.

This is a model-design inference, not identification of anisotropic quantum
polarizability or charge transfer. Existing data do not determine the tensor's
axes, eigenvalues or unique site placement. Nor do they prove that two scalar
donor amplitudes have no feasible remote combination. The evidence warrants
changing the response architecture; it does not yet supply fitted coefficients
or validate the proposed law. Short-range mechanical terms may still need a
separate correction after electrical response is represented. No new fit or
experiment is proposed or executed by this report.
