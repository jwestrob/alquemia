Why the three sectors can be distinguished
At fixed geometry and field-independent h/H, chi=B^T G^-1 B depends on H, not h
or V. The signed moment depends on H and h, but not V. Energy and mechanical
derivatives additionally depend on V. Therefore constrain the sampled H response
first, the signed h response conditional on H second, and the remaining V energy/
mechanics conditional on that electrical response third. These are identifiable
observable roles, not proof that every current tensor/basis coefficient is unique.
A final coupled check must satisfy all groups together; staging is not permission
to discard the back-reaction of electrical corrections on energy or forces.
