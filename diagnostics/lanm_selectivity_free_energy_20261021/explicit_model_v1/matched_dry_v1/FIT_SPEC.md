# Finite paired-ion repair pilot — frozen development protocol

This is a source/preparation declaration. It releases no fit, reference call, validation scoring or dynamics. Root must bind a separate fit release to the actual complete training dataset and its reference review. The reserved 24-cell validation manifest remains unopened until a candidate is frozen. No experimental preference or protein affinity is a target.

## Question and fixed data

Can seven pair-specific Dy parameters reduce the consumed small-cluster discrepancies without absorbing the shared intramolecular distortions or hiding a failed La control? Use exactly 28 training cells: water dimer and aquo8 for both metals (4), three hydrated donor families at −0.15/0/+0.15 Å for both metals (18), and the six matched dry central cells. All coordinates, covalent graphs, multipoles, charges, intrinsic polarizabilities, bonded terms, La parameters, water model and mutual-polarization tolerance remain fixed. There is no ALPB or other implicit solvent.

The reference is the existing **uncorrected** PBE0-D4/def2-TZVP frozen-f ECP energy and gradient surface: La core46 and Dy core55, effective singlets, physical labels La1/Dy6. CP-corrected/CC energies are separate method-sensitivity diagnostics; they are never mixed with uncorrected PBE0 forces as one target surface. Native completion, exact geometry/state/input lineage and finite complete gradients are necessary admission checks. Printed SCF stopping evidence is retained; a missing optional YES/NO residual parse is not a pass. These calculations are consumed development targets, not established physical truth. Reference accuracy, state and small-step derivative qualification remain unestablished. Pending all-electron/CC/CP comparisons can invalidate use of these targets; no parameter fit may silently promote their accuracy.

Before fitting, saved-only preparation reports the six hydrated radial central derivatives and their three paired differences. The derivative is **minus the summed ligand force dotted with the actual ligand translation axis**, with metal/water fixed. Compare against [E(+0.15)−E(−0.15)]/0.30; also report the two endpoint projected slopes. A predeclared development screen flags |difference| > max(1 kcal/mol/Å, 0.10 × max absolute analytic/secant slope). This intentionally coarse, truncation-limited screen is not the prior small-step derivative gate or a proof of analytic correctness. A flagged row blocks automatic fit release and requires a scientific decision; neither deletion nor tolerance adjustment is automatic. All 28 slots remain present.

## Seven parameters and two stages

`FAMILY.json` records initial values and numerical bounds. Pair radii use Tinker's inherited CUBIC-MEAN of DIAMETER values; epsilons use HHG. Overrides use `VDWPR` atom **classes** and `POLPAIR` atom **types**, not interchangeable identifiers.

1. Water: Dy501/water-O class34 radius and epsilon, and Dy501/water-O type36 damping. Radius bounds 0.85–1.15 times inherited pair radius; epsilon 0.25–4 times inherited HHG epsilon; damping 0.05–0.50, initial0.139. Train only on the two water geometry pairs. No donor parameters change.
2. Donors: freeze the water-stage choice. Change Dy501/carboxylate-O class403 radius and type403 damping, and Dy501/amide-O class55 radius and type105 damping. Same radius/damping bounds; both donor epsilons fixed to inherited HHG values. One carboxylate pair serves both coordination modes. No per-geometry parameters or automatic family expansion.

The La amide-carbon override, all other La corrections and all protein multipoles remain fixed. Protein transfer requires the separately frozen complete chemical mapping, including cap carbonyls; it is not part of this fit.

## Observable definitions and exact weights

For pair q define D(q)=E_Dy(q)−E_La(q), and C(q)=D(q)−D(aquo8). Only model-minus-reference residuals enter the loss. Hartree conversion is the consumed runner's 627.509474 kcal/mol. Water-addition residual is [D(wet)−D(matched dry)]_model−[...]_reference. Radial-work residuals use each metal's E(±0.15)−E(0) at fixed composition.

Each molecular body is a connected covalent component: the singleton ion, the complete donor ligand, or one water. Use its **net force**, not mean force, and its torque about its geometric centroid. Divide torque by a fixed 1 Å to express the same force unit. This is a choice of diagnostic scale, not a mass metric or energy attribution. For each fixture, average squared Cartesian residuals within each body; average waters within the water category; then weight present ion/ligand/water categories equally. Within a non-singleton body, translation and rotation receive equal weight; the ion has translation only. Fixtures receive equal weight within each block. Repeat identically for Dy−La response differences. Internal atomic forces are retained in the dataset and diagnostics but are not fitted.

Scale energies by 1 kcal/mol and forces (including torque/1 Å) by 2 kcal/mol/Å. These are development loss scales, not reference error bars. Water loss = 0.50 mean squared C residual + 0.25 absolute Dy rigid-response loss + 0.25 paired rigid-response loss. The aquo anchor's identically zero C is excluded. Donor loss = 0.25 C residuals (12 pairs) + 0.15 water-addition residuals (3) + 0.10 Dy radial-work residuals (6) + 0.25 absolute Dy rigid-response loss + 0.25 paired rigid-response loss. Equal block means prevent a block's number of coordinates from setting its weight.

The energy blocks reuse endpoints and are correlated. These explicit weights are an engineering multi-objective loss, not independent likelihood observations, chi-squared evidence, a covariance estimate, or a confidence interval.

## Independent controls and decision rules

La is not fitted. Report its category-balanced rigid-response RMS and maximum body Cartesian-component error separately for the water and donor stages; thresholds are RMS ≤2 and maximum ≤5 kcal/mol/Å (torque divided by1 Å). Each of its six hydrated radial-work errors must be ≤1 kcal/mol. Failure keeps the paired potential unqualified even if a diagnostic Dy fit succeeds. Absolute La water/donor replacement energies are not inferred from unlike stoichiometries without matched free fragments; this pilot therefore cannot claim absolute binding-energy validation.

For the selected Dy candidate require, separately: every balanced exchange, water-addition and radial-work error ≤1 kcal/mol; absolute Dy and paired rigid-response RMS ≤2 and maximum component ≤5 kcal/mol/Å for each stage. An improvement in scalar objective alone is insufficient. The unchanged La control, reference review, reserved validation, and subsequent candidate force consistency are additional gates. None of these development thresholds establishes physical accuracy or affinity prediction.

Boundary proximity within 1% of any normalized parameter interval and a local sensitivity rank/conditioning flag both prevent automatic qualification. No automatic bound expansion or refit after validation. Parameter uncertainty is not estimated from this deterministic pilot. Local normalized finite-difference sensitivity uses ±0.02 interval units around the final point (clipped at bounds); report column norms, rank at relative1e−6 and condition estimate, with >1e6 flagged. The matrix is of weighted residuals, not a Hessian covariance. Weak identification or a bound hit returns an explicitly limited candidate, not a unique potential.

## Finite execution and cost ceiling

Run a deterministic bounded coordinate pattern search in normalized coordinates: initial step0.10 interval widths; poll − then + for each coordinate; accept the lowest objective in a complete poll, ties keep the earlier point; halve on no improvement; stop below0.00625. Water permits at most25 distinct parameter vectors including its cached initial point, reserving6 vectors for final sensitivity; donors permit49 including its initial point, reserving8 for sensitivity. A partial poll at the cap is recorded and may improve the best point, but is not called converged. No repeated completed parameter/geometry evaluation; exact cache keys include all seven parameters and cell identity.

At most2 Dy cells per water vector and12 per donor vector, each using serial native `analyze E` and analytic `testgrad Y N`: conservative ceiling **1276 native program invocations**, one CPU thread. All La evaluations and original candidate baselines are reused. No new geometry, GFN, MACE, QM, free-fragment or validation calls. First native failure or non-finite output stops the finite run and preserves attempted and unavailable records. Do not restart with a changed solver or epsilon. A single root-owned Slurm allocation runs the pilot; every native invocation records argv, input/output hashes, return code and CPU/wall costs. Native parser/admission and mutual polarization remain unchanged. Candidate force finite differences and reserved validation are separately declared after a candidate freeze; they are not hidden within this budget.

The small pilot permits only a few full polls. A budget-limited best point with residual error does not establish a failed parameter family or converged optimization. Preserve all parameter/geometry cache records for a separately justified continuation; no such continuation is automatic. Even meeting the poll step stopping rule establishes only local numerical stopping, not a global optimum. La control failure blocks physical qualification but remains visible during an explicitly released Dy representability pilot.

## Why this is the next development test

The completed matched-water comparison found AMOEBA water-addition Dy−La energies close in two of three donor families while its paired radial responses and aquo-referenced donor contrasts still disagree. Water-increment agreement alone is therefore insufficient. This pilot keeps both energy cycles and the physically external force response. It cannot fix the large shared ligand internal-force mismatch, establish the frozen-f reference as truth, or release the amidated protein for production sampling.
