Strongest defensible mechanistic conclusion
Within these provisional references, matching a full coordination total can hide
opposing pair and collective errors. The selected model's common-basis total
error -0.3131 combines +3.1483 pair and -3.4614 collective errors; both fixed-shell
paired totals worsen, while consumed trimer regressions also worsen. Static
moment and directional-curvature discrepancies provide independent kinds of
response evidence. A field-independent energy correction cannot repair them
at fixed geometry, so simultaneous conservative energy/force/response constraints
are justified. This does not identify charge transfer, Pauli, polarization,
stiffness location, or a successful functional form. Population schemes disagree
on the small paired charge sign; B contains every nonpair order. The new raw-C
CC agreement cannot remove the remaining uncertainty in those assignments.
