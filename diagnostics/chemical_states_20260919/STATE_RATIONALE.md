# Why test proton location?

The water studies changed orientations and inventory while retaining every water
as neutral H2O and every selected acid as COO−. Their chemical-state vocabulary
therefore could not represent an internally transferred proton. The present test
changes this one assumption at real short contacts; it does not repeat the failed
wide harmonic or finite-basin entropy model.

Alpha-lactalbumin has published proton/metal competition. Its measured calcium
binding changes with pH; early experiments interpreted the acid transition as
competition with several protons. This motivates explicit protonation chemistry,
but **does not prove that a bound hydroxide/carboxylic-acid pair exists**. The latter
is the particular hypothesis tested here, selected by actual source geometry.
[Primary fluorescence/pH experiment](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0006-291X(81)80081-2),
[primary metal-binding study](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0021-9258(18)90595-8).

The original 6IP9 source CIF identifies La-bound bovine alpha-lactalbumin; 1F6S
identifies bovine alpha-lactalbumin. They are structural preparations of one
protein, not an alpha-versus-GGR affinity comparison. Site selection and coordinates
come from the existing source-backed preparation, not fabricated test proteins.

Lanmodulin also motivates state-sensitive selectivity modeling, but our existing
Hans-LanM EF1→EF2→EF3 cores contain no retained waters. We preserve that ordered
vector and do not add unobserved waters merely to run this experiment. The original
Hans study's pH 5 measurements concern coupled protein folding/dimerization, not
three independent site-resolved La/Ca affinity labels.
[Primary Hans-LanM study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-05945-5).
A direct balanced proton-affinity test on its actual carboxylates is a possible
later study, not a calculation claimed here.

Existing `docs/chemical_site_state_v1.md` and
`docs/chemical_site_state_family_v1.md` correctly require explicit composition
and balanced reservoir accounting. This pilot applies those principles to
already-realized, fixed-scaffold QM states and the existing runner. It does not
release the earlier construction-only placement objects for production. The PQQ
microstate registry remains unchanged; dry PQQ preparations are untouched.

For each metal, neutral and transferred configurations have identical atoms and
net charge. In the electronic contrast
`[E_Ca,transfer−E_Ca,neutral]−[E_La,transfer−E_La,neutral]`, no proton, water or aquo
reference energy is needed. No free-energy/entropy correction is silently zero.
Ca and La retain their previously prepared endpoint-specific spectator water H;
we report this actual preparation dependence rather than calling the comparison
an identical-all-coordinate electronic swap. A single proton follows a frozen
continuous path within each endpoint. This is a conditional energy surface, not
an equilibrium state ensemble or a transition-state calculation.
