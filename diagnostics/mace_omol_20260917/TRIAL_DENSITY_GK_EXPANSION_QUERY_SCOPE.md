### Fresh-density archive check (declared before potential queries)

Unlike the saved-density development trial, these geometries have no archived
center-potential observation against which to compare the new query. That
archive-consistency diagnostic is explicitly unavailable; it is not filled with
zero or counted as passed. Native query coordinate/order validation, the same
electric-field/Hessian refinements, charge/projection checks, rigid contraction
and full-model numerical checks remain required with unchanged tolerances.
A duplicate query would not supply independent physical accuracy evidence.
The actual old generating-field interaction is computed from the new density
at its recorded charge coordinates and subtracted once.
