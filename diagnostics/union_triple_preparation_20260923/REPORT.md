All94 originally complete La triples pass source-graph/state preparation; the
same six incomplete triples remain unavailable. Exactly61 triples reproduce the
ten-fold contexts, while33 use genuinely smaller physical atom graphs. This
supports a practical three-source preparation experiment; it does not establish
that the improved ten-fold classifier transfers unchanged.
