Declared after observing partial class overlap, before examining the full-panel
readout decomposition/correlations. This is method-development analysis under
the standing autonomous goal, not a confirmatory test or a new classifier.

Question: which recorded energy readouts carry the medium model's PQQ class
overlap, and is the raw whole-protein contrast associated with total protein
charge more strongly than with the preserved local-core baseline?

Use all25canonical calibration cases once complete, plus all34site scores in
the already completed typed-group transfer. Sources are their actual accepted
endpoint receipts, saved interaction/electrostatic/electron readouts, and exact
source/group preparations. No new molecular evaluations, charge extraction,
geometry, labels, solver or fitting. Crystal reuses remain transfers, outside
the25-case calibration diagnostic. Preserve multisite identities and ordering.

Compute each component contrast with the established Ca-minus-La sign and one
eV-to-model-kcal conversion. Record the remainder between the total contrast
and those three readouts; report whether its range is below the existing0.01
numerical scale without assuming an unverified physical origin. Check the
reconstructed total against the actual reported score.

For the25functional-class cases only, report descriptive Pearson correlations
of each readout/total with full Ca-endpoint charge, number of atoms, selected
metal-group Ca charge, and published baseline S. Also retain full per-case
readouts and each readout's min(La)-max(Ca) class gap. No p-values, flexible
classifier, optimized coefficient, new threshold, outlier exclusion or claim
of independent biological replication. A constant descriptor has correlation
unavailable, not zero. Treat an unassigned remainder constant within0.01 as
numerically unresolved for correlation rather than correlating rounding noise.
The analysis requires all25cases; partial data are not
substituted for a completed panel.

Native readout names locate an algebraic contribution, not a unique biological
cause. In particular, correlation with total charge does not establish a bug,
prove that environment is irrelevant, or validate deleting electrostatics.
