## Question

Does a water model that passes the existing eight consumed water fixtures improve
the already consumed hydrated-donor tests without changing donor parameters?
This extends the previous fixed-candidate transfer to the separately declared
six-parameter water family. No result or candidate is bound at preparation time.

eight water development cells,18 hydrated-donor cells, six unchanged dry controls.

complete declared denominator. No automatic retries or altered numerical settings.

Report the original and new candidate against the same references and criteria,
including all energy and force failures. Water fixtures are development data;
