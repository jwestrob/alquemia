Saved donor dataset — phase 1, 23 October 2026

Authority: ../PHASE_1.txt and the controlling model-form decision. This package
copies admitted saved values and verifies identities; it does not fit parameters,
run a quantum/native program, calculate new reference labels, or claim accuracy.

The explicit shared baseline is common-water17676 plus the selected donor17621
acetate/amide pair overrides. Its complete key and common amoeba09 parameter file
are pinned in materialized_v2/BASELINE.json. Intrinsic metal polarizabilities,
multipoles, bonded/atomic parameters and all non-pair key content are unchanged.
Only applicability of differing pair overrides can establish equivalence: water
uses types36/37 (class34 water O), while donor overrides use types403/105 and
classes403/55. The union receipt verifies all54 water operator fixtures and six
dry donor states. Key hashes alone neither establish nor disprove equivalence.

Eighteen saved hydrated donor states use older water radii/epsilon/damping. They
are materialized, with the exact differences, as INCOMPATIBLE with the shared
baseline. Their quantum targets remain independently labelled reusable evidence;
their classical E/F are not used as shared-baseline residuals. No wet residual or
operator is invented. Historical transfer-model wet-minus-dry cycles are stored
separately and explicitly incompatible, with their signed complete term lists.

Contents
- 24 exact donor geometries: acetate mono/bi and amide, La/Dy, dry central and
  hydrated minus/central/plus. Full saved classical and PBE0-D4 energies/forces,
  atom types/covalent connectivity and complete intact fragment memberships.
- Two 24-dimensional dry monodentate-acetate operators, with alpha, site map,
  unequal Ed/Ep, both native solutions, permanent moment and native nonpolar
  energy. These are zero added h/H; no radial14 correction is carried forward.
- Ten precise common-origin reference moment geometries and saved moment
  derivatives. Charged origin and full signed vectors are preserved.
- Sixteen radial/rotation energy endpoints with exact geometries, native term
  energies, electronic and D4 reference contributions and original two-step
  checks. Endpoint forces are null: those original calls were single points.
- The two central monodentate fixtures include exact center force projections,
  original qualified two-step derivatives, one qualified La susceptibility
  projection and the admitted La canonical CCSD(T) rotation comparison.
  Qualified Dy susceptibility, paired susceptibility and canonical Dy torque
  remain missing. Failed native/reference states are not promoted.

Array contract
All coordinate/fragment indices are zero-based; atom_ids retains native one-based
identity. One acetate contains both O atoms even in its monodentate placement.
Each O frame uses its bonded carbon; water uses its two H atoms symmetrically.
Operator coordinates follow site_atom_indices in site-major xyz order. A0 is
A^-3, alpha A^3, both RHS e/A^2, induced variables e*A, K kcal*A/(mol*e^2).
Permanent and conjugate moments use laboratory origin zero. Original raw arrays
are copied without symmetrization. Nonpolar energy is total minus printed native
polarization, retaining the original printing precision. The immutable kernel
views change metadata names only and attach original saved qualification bounds.

Limitations
Absolute quantum and classical energies have different zeros. No direct absolute
energy subtraction is an interaction label. Complete matching fragment cycles
are required for residual two-/three-body targets; the available donor endpoint
collection does not supply a complete such dataset. Consumed targets retain
PBE0/D4, ECP, state/SCF and reference-method uncertainty. The canonical La torque
supports a specific sampled disagreement, not universal reference truth.
No dA0/dR, dEd/dR, dEp/dR or complete differentiable baseline provider is available.
Holding these arrays fixed can yield only a partial correction derivative, never
a total physical force. No new stability, physical model or periodic admission.

Reproducibility
BUILD_DATA.py --out NEW_DIRECTORY materializes the main dataset from INPUTS.json.
UNION.py --donor DATASET --water WATER_DATASET --out NEW_DIRECTORY proves chemical
applicability and writes metadata-only common-schema kernel views.
DISPLACED.py --baseline BASELINE --out NEW_DIRECTORY materializes saved endpoints.
All runs use one numerical thread, no subprocess/native interface. Outputs are
fresh and never overwritten. READY.json records exact commands, pins and costs.

The original materialized_v1 attempt is preserved with its source and failure
receipt. Its overly strict key-body comparison treated polar-print/save-uinduce
observer flags as physics. The versioned v2 excludes exactly those flags; no
Hamiltonian values or scientific thresholds changed. That failed attempt did
not record reliable process timing; successful run costs are reported separately.
