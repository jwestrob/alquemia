Exact reproduction on these repeatedly inspected sources therefore cannot establish unseen-basin recall or general predictive safety. Any observed untriggered difference or missing route remains visible; the rule is not adjusted after replay. This finite result may motivate a separate prospective efficiency test, not an automatic production change.
