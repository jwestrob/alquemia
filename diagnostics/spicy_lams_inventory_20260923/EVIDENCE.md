# Assay conditions and the four-ion folding claim

## SpyCI-LAMBS readout

The primary article is **Nature Chemical Biology**, not Nature Microbiology as
an older local briefing says. It describes a621-ortholog library. The assay uses
C-terminal SpyTag/GSG constructs immobilized on SpyCatcher-coated porous glass.
The standard block procedure loads25µl of50%resin slurry with1ml containing

## Folding is metal-coupled; four-ion loading is not universal

Mex-LanM has three high-affinity EF1–3 sites and a much weaker, approximately
micromolar EF4. For the110-aa Hans construct, about threeLa/Nd equivalents bind,
but **twoLa/Dy equivalents suffice for the complete CD conformational change**;
the third adds no helicity. La-Hans crystals have threeLa per chain and EF4
modelled asNa. Nd-Mex crystals can occupy all four sites; that sample was loaded
with3.5Nd equivalents before crystallization atpH7. Thus stoichiometry, folding,
EF4 occupation and oligomerization depend on protein, ion and conditions.
A deposited four-metal crystal does not prove that every LanM requires four ions
to fold, or that its state represents thepH3immobilized assay.
[Primary Hans/Mex study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10232371/).

Practical implication: retain full scaffolds and relevant assemblies as potential
future inputs, while declaring site occupancy explicitly. Neither this inventory
nor the existing AF2/Protenix files authorizes an automatic four-ion preparation.
