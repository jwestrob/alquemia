Main result
The reduced full-shell component objective does not transfer uniformly to these consumed metal-two-water cycles. Relative to feca12, all eight individual E3 residual magnitudes and all eight individual mu3 error norms increase. Nevertheless, every paired Dy-minus-La mu3 error norm decreases. The latter improvement is real at the reported contrast level, but results from improved cancellation of larger individual-metal dipole errors. It is not uniform improvement in each metal's response.

What follows for exchange mechanics
The admitted terminal still passes its declared full-shell physical and electrical safeguards. Its stored balanced W9 exchange residual is -0.0756569 kcal/mol, despite opposing P/B Dy-minus-La component residuals. The trimer result shows that this favorable cancellation is not a demonstrated general accuracy property: the four geometry-selected E3 differences retain substantial differential errors and poor energy-sign transfer. Conversely, these four cycles do not overturn the admitted balanced result or prove inaccurate equilibrium exchange. E3 is one three-body contribution, not the full-shell B, total exchange work, solvent free energy, or protein-minus-water selectivity. The eight sampled E3 values cannot be summed to recover B; other triplets and higher orders are absent.

The result therefore separates three statements: full-shell declared gates pass; selected trimer energy differences remain discrepant; selected trimer dipole differences improve mainly through cancellation. None establishes a global infeasibility of the 14-parameter family, identifies the new radial coefficient as the unique cause, or selects a new model. The terminal changes the joint coefficients, so this comparison cannot isolate an e-only causal effect. The main scientific value is a concrete warning that additional reduction of the same fitted objective is not by itself evidence of transfer improvement.
