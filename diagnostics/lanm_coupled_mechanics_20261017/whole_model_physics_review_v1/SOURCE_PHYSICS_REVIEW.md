# Native whole-MACE physics review

## Finding

The completed2049-atom Mex panel uses a **finite-range learned atom-energy model with global charge/multiplicity labels**, plus separately added fixed-charge ALPB. It has no explicit long-range vacuum Coulomb sum, self-consistent charge equilibration or induced-dipole polarization in the executed MACE energy path. Whole-system scoring removes the former electronic/MM partition from this model evaluation; it does not establish complete protein electrostatics or accurate La/Dy mechanics.

This review reads code and saved artifacts only. Nine relevant installed source files exactly match the archived backend source inventory. Exact evidence hashes are in [SOURCE_PINS.json](SOURCE_PINS.json). The checkpoint was not loaded and no numerical calculations or jobs were run.

* **Inference from this code:** one final atom-energy contribution depends on at most three graph hops (an18Å maximum path length through6Å edges). This is not a universal18Å force cutoff. Total forces differentiate the sum of overlapping atom-energy neighborhoods; coupling of two atoms through one such contribution can span up to six graph hops. These are dependency bounds, not measured distances or proof of accurate response at those ranges. Geometry changes beyond the finite graph dependency cannot create an explicit long-range vacuum field response at fixed global labels.
* **Cutoff behavior:** the radial block computes a polynomial envelope. When radial `apply_cutoff` is false, it returns that envelope separately; the nonlinear interaction multiplies its weights and local edge-density contribution by it. Thus that flag does not disable the finite cutoff. Smooth edge suppression and a finite receptive field are compatible. See [radial block](/groups/banfield/projects/environmental/sr/srvp2020/Jacob/lanthanide_binding/on_density_scanner/alchemical_bvs/workspaces/mace_hybrid_20260916/software_v1/venv/lib/python3.11/site-packages/mace/modules/blocks.py:390), [nonlinear interaction](/groups/banfield/projects/environmental/sr/srvp2020/Jacob/lanthanide_binding/on_density_scanner/alchemical_bvs/workspaces/mace_hybrid_20260916/software_v1/venv/lib/python3.11/site-packages/mace/modules/blocks.py:1300), [polynomial envelope](/groups/banfield/projects/environmental/sr/srvp2020/Jacob/lanthanide_binding/on_density_scanner/alchemical_bvs/workspaces/mace_hybrid_20260916/software_v1/venv/lib/python3.11/site-packages/mace/modules/radial.py:113).

## What charge and spin mean here

The saved checkpoint has categorical graph inputs: total charge (201 slots, offset100) and total spin/multiplicity (101 slots), each with1024 embedding dimensions. `GenericJointEmbedding` looks up these labels, broadcasts each graph's labels to every atom, concatenates/project them, and adds the result to initial node features. Subsequent local interactions and readouts can therefore change throughout the system when the global label changes. The checkpoint also has an embedding-energy readout, evidenced in the saved same-checkpoint result; its coordinate-independent offset is distinct from geometry-dependent response. See [embedding implementation](/groups/banfield/projects/environmental/sr/srvp2020/Jacob/lanthanide_binding/on_density_scanner/alchemical_bvs/workspaces/mace_hybrid_20260916/software_v1/venv/lib/python3.11/site-packages/mace/modules/embeddings.py:7), [executed ScaleShiftMACE path](/groups/banfield/projects/environmental/sr/srvp2020/Jacob/lanthanide_binding/on_density_scanner/alchemical_bvs/workspaces/mace_hybrid_20260916/software_v1/venv/lib/python3.11/site-packages/mace/modules/models.py:490).

The actual Mex batch has total charge+2 and aggregate multiplicities La10/Dy15 or La4/Dy9. The task name “one Nd reversed” denotes an aggregate conditioning hypothesis: **no per-ion spin vectors, reversal identity, local magnetic moments, exchange coupling or SOC are supplied**. The code cannot distinguish which Nd is reversed when geometry/species and total label are identical. Likewise, a passed charge-label check is not a predicted atomic charge distribution or an oxidation-state constraint. See [actual panel](/groups/banfield/projects/environmental/sr/srvp2020/Jacob/lanthanide_binding/on_density_scanner/alchemical_bvs/workspaces/lanm_coupled_mechanics_20261017/mex_whole_collection_v1/PANEL.py:21), [batch admission](/groups/banfield/projects/environmental/sr/srvp2020/Jacob/lanthanide_binding/on_density_scanner/alchemical_bvs/workspaces/lanm_series_followup_20260923/prepared_v2/implementation/mace_omol.py:280).

The prior [native charge-embedding audit](/groups/banfield/projects/environmental/sr/srvp2020/Jacob/lanthanide_binding/on_density_scanner/alchemical_bvs/workspaces/mace_omol_20260917/charge_embedding_audit_v1/result.json) already records nontrivial charge categories, including+2; it does not establish training coverage. The [all-charge-identical ablation](/groups/banfield/projects/environmental/sr/srvp2020/Jacob/lanthanide_binding/on_density_scanner/alchemical_bvs/workspaces/mace_omol_20260917/charge_ablation_component_v3/result.json) deliberately zeroed a contribution and must not be described as native behavior. No repetition of that consumed diagnosis is proposed.

## Which physics is explicit, implicit or absent

`ScaleShiftMACE.forward` forms local features, sums atomic energy readouts, scales/shifts them, and differentiates the energy for forces. It does not solve for electron density, charges, dipoles or a self-consistent global field. Local electrostatics, bonding and polarization-like responses can be learned implicitly from training energies; their presence or accuracy for this mixed-Nd protein is not demonstrated by the code. Installing `graph-longrange`, or having dielectric/dipole classes elsewhere in the package, does not activate them in this forward path. See [full forward](/groups/banfield/projects/environmental/sr/srvp2020/Jacob/lanthanide_binding/on_density_scanner/alchemical_bvs/workspaces/mace_hybrid_20260916/software_v1/venv/lib/python3.11/site-packages/mace/modules/models.py:443) and [native loader and invocation](/groups/banfield/projects/environmental/sr/srvp2020/Jacob/lanthanide_binding/on_density_scanner/alchemical_bvs/workspaces/lanm_coupled_response_20261016/lbt3_fd_gpu_v1/LBT3_FD.py:80).

The panel adds archived ALPB energy/gradient using unchanged ff19SB/TIP3P charges and four formal+3 ions. This supplies a geometry-dependent, nonlocal continuum-solvation term, but its charges do not relax with MACE and its reaction field is not fed back into MACE. It is not a replacement for omitted self-consistent vacuum electrostatics. No extra dispersion or other long-range adapter was enabled. See [solvent admission](/groups/banfield/projects/environmental/sr/srvp2020/Jacob/lanthanide_binding/on_density_scanner/alchemical_bvs/workspaces/lanm_coupled_mechanics_20261017/mex_whole_collection_v1/PANEL.py:104) and [composite assembly](/groups/banfield/projects/environmental/sr/srvp2020/Jacob/lanthanide_binding/on_density_scanner/alchemical_bvs/workspaces/lanm_coupled_mechanics_20261017/mex_whole_collection_v1/PANEL.py:287).

## Consequence for the mechanics question

A coherent whole-system learned energy can improve partition consistency while still missing remote charge redistribution and field-dependent scaffold response. Global spin sensitivity may also change distant local forces through broadcast conditioning, without demonstrating a physically localized magnetic rearrangement. These are consequential model limitations to keep separate from a coding/counting defect; they do not by themselves identify the cause or size of the observed mechanics error.

The completed [eight-cell panel](/groups/banfield/projects/environmental/sr/srvp2020/Jacob/lanthanide_binding/on_density_scanner/alchemical_bvs/workspaces/lanm_coupled_mechanics_20261017/mex_whole_collection_v1/execution/RESULT.json) establishes finite native outputs and conditional model sensitivity, not physical accuracy, spin localization or protein-scale transfer. Same-coordinate independent force/work references remain the discriminating evidence. This review creates no new acceptance gate or model change. An arbitrary added Coulomb term would also require a defined separation from learned local interactions; the absence of an explicit term is not permission to double-count it.
