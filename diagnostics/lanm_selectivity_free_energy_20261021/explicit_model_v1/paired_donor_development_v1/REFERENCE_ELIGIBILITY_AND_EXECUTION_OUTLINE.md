## Decisions still needed before freezing an execution specification

**Reference scope.** The substantive choice is whether all28 consistent PBE0-D4 rows are accepted as *provisional development targets*, with the sampled central amide qualification and remaining force/state limits stated, or whether selected additional donor directions must be numerically qualified first. The new test removes the specific central-amide objection but cannot settle panel-wide eligibility. Recommended next declaration is provisional development on the existing panel, without claiming universal force qualification; require further reference work only for a specific unresolved discrepancy. Do not silently exclude acetamide, replace CP energies alone, or loosen a target after fitting.

**Finite budget.** Choose the number `N` of additional joint iterations before execution. The outline entails at most `20N` new parameter vectors. A single-metal carboxylate probe changes8 donor cells; a single-metal amide probe changes4. Thus a complete derivative batch costs at most `8×8×2 + 8×4×2 =192` native programs, and four full trials at most `4×24×2 =192`, totaling **384N native programs** before exact reuse. For scale,12 iterations would mean240 vectors and4608 programs; this is a budget example, not a release or a chosen cap. Eight one-thread workers can process the16 derivative vectors in batches. Every program count includes analyze plus analytic `testgrad Y N`; these parameter probes do not include Cartesian finite-difference energy evaluations. Later numerical qualification has its own separately declared count.

The reference-scope declaration and finite cap are scientific/resource choices, not new standing approval machinery. Root remains submission owner. There is no automatic extension, epsilon expansion, bound change, reserved24 access or production promotion.
