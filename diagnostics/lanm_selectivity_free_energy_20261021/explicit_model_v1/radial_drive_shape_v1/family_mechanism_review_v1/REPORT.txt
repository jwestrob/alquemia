1. What the fourteen parameters actually control

Per metal, the family has a,b (drive), c,d (metal stiffness), native buffered-14-7
R/epsilon, and e (radial drive shape). The fitted u/v box coordinates map to
c=ln(4)(u+v)/4, d=ln(4)(u-v)/4; they are not the induced dipoles below. [E01,E02]

With r_i=R_Oi-R_M, fixed overlap f_i and damping phi_i,
  h_Oi = 3 f_i phi_i [a+b eta_i+e psi_i] r_i,
  psi_i=(|r_i|^2-r0^2)/r0^2, r0=2.50 Angstrom;
  H_MM = {exp[g(s)(c+d eta)]-1} I3/alpha_M.
Here s=sum f_i, g=2s/(1+s), eta_i=(s-f_i)/(1+s-f_i), and
eta=(s^2-sum f_i^2)/(1+s^2). All other added H blocks are zero. T, intrinsic
alpha, permanent multipoles, native damping and water interactions stay fixed.
The response equations are (A0+H)u=Ed0+h and (A0+H)v=Ep0+h, with stationary
energy -K/2 u·(Ep0+h). Preserve unequal dual RHSs; no Ed=Ep assumption is needed.
The new gradient includes K/2[u·dH·v-(u+v)·dh] alongside the native derivative.
These are coupled response modifications, not independent energy offsets. [E01,E02]

In a single-water dimer, eta_i=eta=0: b,d are inactive, while a,c,e,R/epsilon
can change the dimer. Thus a purely collective addition vanishing in dimers
cannot directly repair P or those single-water force controls. Metal-only H is
isotropic and rank at most three for one metal, but changes all coupled dipoles
through the inverse operator. It is not equivalent to changing only a metal
observable. There is no new water-site stiffness, off-diagonal H, anisotropic
correction or independent angular descriptor. Baseline tensors and vector
orientations already carry angular dependence; this is not an angle-free model.
At equal metal-O radii, the added scalar environment functions cannot separately
encode near/far water angle, although the full coupled response can differ. [E01,E10]

2. Why P/B, force, dipole and susceptibility gains need separate interpretation

P=sum_i[E(MWi)-E(M)-E(Wi)], C=E(M+shell)-E(M)-E(shell), B=C-P. The targets here
are two Dy-minus-La W8/W9 contrasts, with the admitted common-parent electronic
basis plus original D4 cancellation. B contains all nonpair orders, not uniquely
three-body energy, polarization or charge transfer. Earlier fitted coordination
error -0.3131 kcal/mol was +3.1483 pair minus3.4614 collective: good C can hide
bad P/B, and W9-W8 subtraction can additionally hide shared shell-size errors.
Per-parent D4-complete targets were unavailable; do not invent them. [E03]

A field-independent pair energy (including R/epsilon) cancels directly from B
and the seven-term E3 at fixed matched geometry; it cannot change dipoles/chi.
An unchanged internal-water contribution likewise cancels from these balanced
interaction cycles and fixed-internal-geometry rigid work. In contrast, the
pair-additive radial term is additive in h, not in the solved scalar energy:
through (A0+H)^-1 it can affect collective B at general geometries. [E01,E02,E07]

At ideal r0, e contributes zero h but a nonzero radial Jacobian
6 e phi(r0) n n^T. With all old coefficients fixed, it leaves central energy and
moments unchanged while changing radial forces. Literal printed coordinates
are never clamped to r0. The admitted local panel had exactly zero added P/B
Jacobian columns, yet nonzero original-residual columns: joint14 gains came by
allowing old parameters to move while compensating derivative costs. e alone
is not a new central W9 signed-dipole direction. [E02,E11]

Conditional susceptibility is proportional to W^T(A0+H)^-1 W. Changing a,b,e
cannot change it at any fixed geometry and fixed c,d; R/epsilon also cannot.
Changing c,d can. This statement is exact for the stated linear functional,
not a claim that a joint14 fit must leave chi unchanged. Four consumed lab-z
dimer/far-trimer state responses and their derived trimer-minus-dimer increments
are neither a complete response tensor nor independent seven-term alpha3/B
measurements, relaxed-geometry response or thermodynamic susceptibility. [E01,E08]

  amplitude was selected. This rejects those fixed perturbations, not every

4. What remains unproven

Independent angular/anisotropic or water/off-diagonal stiffness freedom is absent
from this14-parameter form, but no global inadequacy theorem follows. An energy-
only patch cannot directly fix electrical observables; a collective-only patch
cannot directly fix dimers. Those are fixed-scope algebraic statements, not a
choice of the next model. A failed finite trial only locates a tradeoff along
its tested direction. Preserve exact terminal force margins, parameter bounds,
finite stopping budget, solver statuses and signed electrical results when root
integrates20605. None of the evidence here establishes reference truth, transfer,
periodic dynamics or binding free-energy accuracy. No new experiment is proposed
or released by this synthesis.
