## Physical/state contract

Requests pin the three source files, source configuration, metal/PQQ/site-role
selectors and the frozen three-La-membership reference. Actual protein sequence,
numbering, site roles, assembly and selected La identity must agree. Source IDs
are sorted before preparation; the first is the physical-state anchor. It has no
biological-label or calibration role. Experimental labels are neither required
nor used to construct the geometry; supplied unresolved evidence/provenance is
retained.

The fixed fragment union comes from exactly those three local preparations.
Complete selected source/cofactor atoms, hydrogen parents, caps, retained bonds,
formal charges, multiplicities, cofactor microstates and explicit water inventory
must match across all three. Each source keeps its own physical coordinates.
Every Ca/La pair and source-connected Kinematics mapping replays q0. The two
reference triples reproduce the original state signatures and coordinates
exactly; final source/configuration/implementation pins are in RESULT.json.

The actual four-mode selector requires paired native origin forces; these were
not invented or borrowed. The preparation exposes all physical modes and six
paired origin tasks. A later existing-executor adapter can use that handoff for
six origin evaluations, six bounded searches, at most six cross-MACE evaluations
and36 native GFN2 cells per complete triple. Those calls are **not launched** here.
No execution subcommand or new workflow engine was introduced.

## What ran and what did not

All fixture operations reused exact existing source preparations, then rebuilt
the source-backed fragment graph/maps. No new protonation, folds, energies,
forces, geometry optimization or Slurm jobs. Final five fixture preparations
took23.037s combined local wall time; development preparations/tests/reporting
are additional unmetered CPU work. V1 reference preparation drafts remain;
fixtures_v2 and plm_v1 are the final pinned versions with immutable code snapshots.
The implemented `fresh` preparation option reuses the existing protonator, but
was not exercised by this preparation-only delivery.

Six actual-fixture tests pass: label/canonical-free requests, original reference
state/geometry replay, both real PLM paired maps, actual missing-source handling,
and explicit corrupted copies testing membership/anchor/reference rejection.
No fake successful molecular result was used. The exact frozen threefold
reference is pinned; no band inheritance from a tenfold model or refit occurs.
