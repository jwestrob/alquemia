MODEL-FORM DECISION: INDEPENDENT FUNCTIONAL REVIEW
23 October 2026. Saved sources/results only; no new calculations or execution.

Recommendation

Choose B: an explicit geometry-resolved short-range two-/three-body residual
energy, with conjugate electrical response, atop a fixed long-range AMOEBA
baseline. Give zero-field energy and its geometry dependence their own freedom
instead of forcing every missing contribution through quadratic induction.
Donor-local angular h/H (A) is a possible response component of that architecture,
not an established explanation of all quantum three-body residuals. A simple
positive radial oxygen spring was already tested as EXCHPOL. This choice is
a development decision based on persistent coupled errors, not a proof that
every parameter choice in the old form is impossible or that charge transfer,
exchange or penetration uniquely causes those errors. No fit or run follows.

1. Exact implemented functional

Write u=uind, v=uinp, K=electric/dielec and A0=alpha^-1-T. Permanent multipoles,
native direct/exclusion fields Ed0/Ep0, bonded terms and buffered-14-7 vdW
remain separate native terms. The implemented response satisfies

 L = K/2 [u^T(A0+H)v - u^T(Ep0+h) - v^T(Ed0+h)],
 (A0+H)u=Ed0+h,  (A0+H)v=Ep0+h,
 U_pol* = -K/2 u^T(Ep0+h).

This dual functional is stationary, not a joint minimum in independent u,v.
The physical response operator requires stability. Explicit derivatives are
K/2[u^T(dH)v-(u+v)^Tdh], plus all native tensor, field and local-frame terms.
Both native channels are retained; they are not independent electronic species.
For equal direct fields this reduces to the usual single induced-dipole minimum.

The radial14 correction is, for metal M and water oxygen i,

 h_i = 3 f_i phi_i [a_M+b_M eta_i+e_M psi_i] R_i,
 H_MM = (exp(sigma)-1) I/alpha_M; all other added H blocks are zero,
 s=sum_i f_i; eta_i=sum_(j!=i)f_j/[1+sum_(j!=i)f_j],
 eta=2 sum_(i<j)f_i f_j/(1+s^2); sigma=2s/(1+s)*(c_M+d_M eta),
 psi_i=(r_i^2-r0^2)/r0^2; r0=2.50 Angstrom,
 f(r)=[(1+2r+(2r)^2/3)exp(-2r)]^2 / same_value_at_r0,
 phi=(1-exp(-kappa r^3))/r^3, with its finite contact continuation.

Thus the four response coefficients per metal separate pair/collective drive
from pair/collective isotropic METAL stiffness. Two radial-drive coefficients
and four metal-O R/epsilon values complete the fourteen parameters. The e term
adds independent radial slope at r0 but vanishes there in value; it leaves the
fixed-geometry susceptibility operator unchanged. Literal radii are not clamped.
The periodic compact switch/PME implementation is a controlled numerical route
for this law, not a new electronic-response mechanism.

2. Present versus absent degrees of freedom

Present: permanent site charges/dipoles/quadrupoles and their frame orientation;
mutually coupled induced dipoles on the represented polarizable sites; angular
dipole tensors; arbitrarily high induction orders through the coupled solve;
smooth radial coordination dependence of h/H; independent La/Dy parameters.
It is already a many-body, polarizable model. 'Add polarization' or 'add many
body' does not specify a missing mechanism.

Absent from the active radial14 law: additional donor-site anisotropic stiffness;
added off-diagonal response blocks; independent donor-frame/ligand-angle
descriptors in the correction amplitudes; geometry-dependent permanent charge
redistribution or variational charge flow; short-range charge penetration;
induced quadrupoles; electronic saturation/nonlinear response at fixed geometry.
Water intrinsic alpha, permanent multipoles and native T stay fixed. Existing
library features are not active merely because their routines exist: the
qualified vacuum route rejects EXCHPOL, charge flux, penetration and mixed
direct/mutual-pair-response declarations.

For equal-r two-water trimers, added scalar amplitudes depend only on the two
radii: h uses a+b/2 and sigma=(4/3)(c+2d/5) at ideal r0. Near/far angles still
enter R_i and native T; the WHOLE energy is not angle-independent. What is
missing is an independently adjustable angular dependence of the correction.
The effective metal inverse polarizability is exp(sigma)/alpha_M, isotropically.

3. What earlier alterations actually changed

- R/epsilon, intrinsic alpha and one POLPAIR gamma: reparameterization within
  their existing pair/linear-dipole laws. A larger box is not a new mechanism.
- Separate direct/mutual gamma: a real generalization of radial damping, still
  within the point-dipole response class. The four +/-5% probes were active but
  weak and traded near/far response; none repaired the consumed panel.
- Geometry-dependent h and metal H: a different effective interaction law from
  unmodified AMOEBA, yet still quadratic electronic response with a very small
  spatial basis. Pair/collective freedom is already present.
- The radial e basis: not merely a relabelled old coefficient, but an additional
  radial function in the same drive space; no new electronic variable, angular
  descriptor or susceptibility direction. Further radial bases would remain
  refinements of this strategy.
- EXCHPOL: genuinely distributed tensor stiffness. The tested law increased
  oxygen radial inverse susceptibility by q*f(r)*n*n^T/alpha_O, leaving the
  tangential response and metal tensor unchanged. Shared positive q=.01/.05
  worsened C/B and original gates. Those results do not exclude independently
  driven, signed, angular/environment-dependent donor tensors, but must not be
  advertised as an untested simple oxygen-stiffness rescue.

4. What the evidence supports

The original susceptibility panel found excess added-water chi_zz despite low
zero-field pair moments and insufficient collective suppression. That rejects
a uniform 'all polarizabilities are too small' explanation for the measured
mode. It does not fix every tensor direction or every later candidate: feca12
has negative La state errors (-.8791/-.9391 bohr^3) and positive Dy errors
(+.3322/+.2949), with much smaller conditional-increment errors. At fixed
geometry h changes equilibrium moments without changing chi; H changes both.
That distinction was already implemented, not merely proposed.

The radial14 terminal improves P/B RMS to9.8669/8.2866 kcal/mol and passes all
25 original gates, but W9 Dy-minus-La z dipole remains +.127883 D versus
-.104618 D. Against feca12, all eight consumed individual E3 and mu3 errors
worsen. All four paired E3 signs are wrong; W9 far is -2.27813 versus +.43004
kcal/mol. Paired dipole errors improve through cancellation of larger
individual errors. These patterns expose poor transfer of the chosen objective,
not a reliable thermodynamic model or a global infeasibility proof.

The trimer local-span test found only a near-boundary ~.74% reduction and no
admitted positive feasible witness (AlmostSolved; exact q cap violated). It
is not an optimizer-independent impossibility theorem. Prior no-worsening
score ceilings were development restrictions, not physical accuracy bounds.

Saved E3 changes appear in the native 'Polarization' component because the
changed terms act through the induced solution and pair-additive nonpolar
terms cancel from these matched cycles. That locates the CLASSICAL change.
It does not assign the QUANTUM residual to induction, exchange or charge
transfer. B=C-P contains all nonpair orders; it is not uniquely polarization
and cannot be reconstructed from eight sampled E3 values. Original common-
basis PBE0/ECP electronic targets, D4-inclusive development targets and their
SCF/state limitations remain distinct. This review consumes no new reference
outcomes and makes no numerical-precision explanation for their discrepancies.

5. Concrete alternative laws and their limits

A. Narrower alternative: replace the metal-only isotropic
correction by donor-local symmetric tensors whose principal directions follow
the water/donor frame and a smooth angular neighbour descriptor, with an
independent covariant local drive. For example, a tensor may distinguish the
M-O direction from the water bisector and respond to the surrounding ligand
orientation tensor. This can alter response modes that a rank-three scalar
metal block cannot address and can distinguish ligand arrangements at equal
radial coordination. It remains in the variational dipole class: no claim of
new charge-transfer physics follows. Simply setting H_O=q*f*n*n^T is the old
EXCHPOL hypothesis; changing only its amplitude is not this architecture.
Stability of each local block alone would not ensure full coupled stability.
This is real new spatial freedom, but it still sends all missing zero-field
energy through quadratic polarization. The data have not isolated an anisotropic
response mode that explains the E3 errors; choosing A alone risks continuing
the same energy/force/electrical tradeoff with more coefficients.

B. Recommended parent architecture: use explicit, symmetric,
finite-range metal-ligand two-body and metal-ligand-ligand three-body energy
surfaces, with angular dependence and field-conjugate response. Its new degrees
of freedom are independent pair energy values/radial slopes, nonadditive
ligand-angle/coordination energy, covariant dipole vectors and symmetric response
tensors, all derivatives of one scalar energy. Locally its uniform-field
expansion is DeltaU=V(R)-F dot m(R)-1/2 F^T alpha(R) F. This makes clear
why a scalar V alone can repair E3/forces but cannot repair dipoles or chi.
For a transferable polarizable Hamiltonian, these response derivatives must
enter a single distributed electrostatic/variational energy in the actual
environment, not be appended as separately predicted observables. The written
uniform-field expansion specifies observables around an isolated state; it is
NOT by itself a transferable bulk potential. A complete law must specify local
field/potential coupling, mutual response, charge conservation, smooth fragment
separation and all coordinate/field derivatives. Rotations and equivalent-ligand
permutations must preserve the scalar energy. Define residual targets against
one fixed baseline to avoid counting its induction again. An angular donor h/H
law can supply part of this response while V supplies independent missing energy.
Three-body
terms vanish in dimers and cannot fix P; a two-body sector is also necessary
when pair errors remain. This is a different interaction law, not another
amplitude multiplying eta. Sparse consumed trimers neither identify it uniquely
nor suffice to train or validate its geometric and environmental dependence.

C. Variational, charge-conserving short-range charge flow is a genuinely new
electronic degree of freedom, coupled to dipoles in the same stationary energy.
It can redistribute fragment charge and its associated multipoles. Present
energy/dipole/chi evidence does not establish its necessity; partition-dependent
atomic populations would not prove it either. Unconstrained global charge
equilibration risks spurious long-range transfer, so fragment separation and
charge conservation are essential. It adds a stronger microscopic assumption
than the recommended effective residual for the current
evidence. Nonlinear electronic saturation is likewise untested, not diagnosed
by one small-field linear susceptibility discrepancy.

6. Thermodynamic decision

The goal is protein-versus-water La/Dy exchange free energy, not the fit of one
cluster C contrast or a native component label. At a common standard state this
requires the protein alchemical Dy-minus-La free energy minus its solution
counterpart, including the ensembles and any restraint/chemical-state terms.
A viable replacement must describe donor/water competition, coordination
changes, solvent screening and forces with the same conservative Hamiltonian.
Water-only radial fits and finite periodic parity cannot establish these.

Choose B explicitly, preserve the current
model as a baseline, and judge evidence across individual and paired energies,
signed dipoles, susceptibility modes and forces. Do not use improved cancellation
or force-code correctness as a physical-accuracy claim. No new fit, quantum
calculation, probe, numerical replay, build or launch is requested or performed
by this review. Exact inspected sources and saved outcome authorities are in
EVIDENCE.json; this report recommends model design, not production promotion.
