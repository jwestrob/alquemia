# Next physical decision: use actual energy curves, not a spring extrapolation

Status: conditional research direction, **no new calculation launched by this note**.
The native La/differential response checks in1203771 determine the next action.

## What the existing evidence supports

The native DFT control displacements agree with the composite. The first large
PLM calcium response also agrees: moving the compressed Asp outward substantially
lowers energy. La response remains unvalidated. The three-fold PLM experiment
shows that choosing another saved fold alone does not eliminate this concern.
Neither PLM target has an experimental metal-specificity label.

The existing composite profile diagnostics show another important limitation:
for the compressed extra-Asp paths, the estimated curvature changes substantially
between the0.2 and0.4radian sampling intervals, and the lowest sampled energy is
at the positive boundary for both metals. The present domain does not contain a
demonstrated minimum. A quadratic spring extrapolation outside it would therefore
add unsupported information, even if the small native displacements agree.

## Why a nonlinear response is the useful next hypothesis

A single compressed geometry can penalize the larger metal. A physically allowed
donor adjustment may change that penalty differently for Ca and La. The relevant
quantity is the difference between their independently calculated accommodation
energies, not a chosen target distance, a fitted spring or a step chosen to make
an unknown PLM prediction La-like.

If native La/differential work agrees, the next candidate should evaluate actual
composite energies along meaningful whole-donor coordinates and establish bounded
stationary behavior. It must first resolve the existing boundary minimum and
nonlinear profile; a local harmonic correction is not yet justified. New angle
extent, coupling, step acceptance and validation points must be recorded in a
separate experiment before execution. The completed profile remains immutable.

The candidate must then be tested with one common rule on known-label PQQ
structures, including their structural variants. Keeping the established PQQ
task reliable is a requirement. A movement-induced change in an unknown PLM
classification cannot serve as an accuracy target or calibration label. Any new
response score/bands remain a separate protocol; the baseline stays accessible.

If native La response disagrees materially, first identify whether the discrepancy
comes from native MACE, solvent transfer, geometry mapping, or electronic state.
Do not hide it with fitted coefficients or expand a geometry search to obtain a
desired call. Ca agreement alone does not qualify differential relaxation.
