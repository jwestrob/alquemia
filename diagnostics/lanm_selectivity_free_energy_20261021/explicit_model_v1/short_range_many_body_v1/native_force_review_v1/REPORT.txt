Independent native force-bridge source audit — 23 October 2026

Conclusion
For the bounded vacuum MUTUAL/Thole baseline, the existing epolar1a Cartesian
loops can supply the missing baseline derivatives at externally supplied u/v.
They do not require a second induction solve. This is a source conclusion,
conditional on the setup/guards below; no compiled or numerical bridge has been
qualified by this review. Exact parent pins are in EVIDENCE.json.

Functional and signs
L0 = K/2 [u^T A0 v - u^T Ep0 - v^T Ed0], K=electric/dielec.
At fixed supplied u/v its coordinate derivative is
K/2 [u^T (dA0) v - u^T dEp0 - v^T dEd0].
The source epolar1a uses f=.5*electric/dielec (line237). Its direct-channel
section (848–869) multiplies dscale/dEd by uinp=v and pscale/dEp by uind=u.
The MUTUAL/Thole section916–953 adds the two-channel interaction derivative.
No stationarity substitution is needed in those explicit force loops. Native
polarity is geometry-independent in this restricted baseline; the self diagonal
therefore has no missing coordinate derivative. A geometry-dependent new H is
handled by the new kernel, not by changing native alpha.

Use native gradient + dV + K/2[u^T(dH)v - (u+v)^T dh], with u/v detached when
differentiating the explicit correction. Native derivs and dep are gradients;
reported forces are their negatives. Do not subtract the baseline polarization
gradient: the native clone has already evaluated it at the supplied new u/v.
Do not include the full kernel gradient and this explicit correction twice.

Energy is different from force bookkeeping
epolar1a calls epolar1e before its force loops (line204). epolar1e (8445–8485)
returns the reduced expression -K/2 u·Ep0 via udirp/alpha. That is not the full
new stationary energy at a corrected u/v. It may remain as a scratch diagnostic,
but native_total-minus-ep is U_other and must be combined with the complete new
dual functional. The cloned gradient returns dU_other+dL0, despite reporting
that reduced scratch energy. Labels must make this distinction explicit.

Initialization required
1. Original initial/getxyz/mechanic must complete for each fresh geometry/key.
   mechanic initializes covalent topology/exclusions, multipole axes, local
   multipoles, polarizabilities, pdamp, jpolar/thlval and polarization groups.
   kpolar allocates udir/udirp/uind/uinp (215–233) and constructs douind/ipole.
   Merely loading coordinates and four dipole arrays is insufficient.
2. Rotate permanent multipoles with chkpole/rotpole before direct-field export.
   dfield0a computes unequal raw Ed0/Ep0 and initializes local field/scaling
   scratch itself (induce.f667–790); preserve dpequal and all topology scales.
3. Zero all n-atom dipole/direct arrays, then map site-major supplied u/v through
   exact ipole. Require the fixture's complete site map, finite positive alpha,
   active douind and the declared dense-operator scope. Set udir=alpha*Ed0 and
   udirp=alpha*Ep0, not alpha times the new h-corrected RHS. The latter would
   change the scratch energy and conceal which functional was evaluated.
4. Require MUTUAL, use_thole, dielec=1 for current Python K=electric, or explicitly
   propagate electric/dielec. Reject unsupported bounds/replica/Ewald/list,
   solvation/Born/reaction-field, charge penetration/flux, alternate direct
   damping, exchange polarization, frequency-dependent/extended-Lagrangian and
   predictor paths. Retain only declared native terms/atom types.
5. Explicitly require or initialize tcgnab=0 in this MUTUAL-only isolated entry.
   epolar1a reads TCG scratch in an unconditional j=1,tcgnab loop (264–277),
   even though its actual TCG force branch is guarded. Do not rely on accidental
   fresh-process storage values. OPT/TCG arrays need not otherwise be populated.
6. Induce's predictor-history update, debug printing and matrix-export observer
   are unnecessary for this one-shot no-solve bridge. Their absence must not be
   misreported as an induction solve or admitted export. Any ufield operator
   construction must restore or overwrite all trial uind/uinp before the force
   call. Geometry, topology, alpha, damping and field must remain unchanged
   between field/operator export, Python solve and supplied-state contraction.

Torques and nonpolar retention
epolar1a builds ufld/dufld from BOTH channels, converts permanent-multipole
torques with torque(i,...,dep) (2417), and accumulates frame virial (2418–2449).
These are essential on acetate/amide; direct pair forces alone omit frame
derivatives. chkpole is idempotent at an unchanged geometry because it flips the
yaxis sign and corresponding local pole components together. The later native
empole1 rotation therefore does not create a second physical rotation.

The gradient clone must preserve original allocation/zeroing, all bonded,
permanent electrostatic, VDW and other admitted terms, final sum and watfour2
handling. Replacing only its epolar1 dispatch is the narrow route. Alternatively
splitting U_other must retain all original components; subtracting a cached old
polarization force from a changed geometry is invalid. Four-site water and
unreviewed coupled extra terms should remain outside the finite fixture scope.
gradient.f180–284 contains these initialization,
dispatch and final accumulation stages.

External-field interface hazard — resolve before implementation freeze
Native induce adds texfld to both direct fields, and empole1's wrapper invokes
exfield1 to add permanent-field energy/frame forces. The Python kernel separately
adds its passed field to Ed0/Ep0 and -K*mu_perm·F to its energy. Feeding native
field-inclusive RHS/U_other and then passing F again would double-count it.
The minimal first bridge can require use_exfld=false. If field support is
included, export explicitly zero-field RHS/U_other, or define and check another
single-count convention, while ensuring native force and Python solve see the
same field. No extra permanent-field term should be appended to the native
force that already includes exfield1.
