## Source disulfide repair

The original rule explicitly rejects Cys. Q88JH0 has Cys131–132 at2.102Å and
A8R3S4 Cys139–140 at2.174Å, both with two CB hydrogens and no sulfur hydrogen.
Re-reading the original protonated PDBs with the same installed OpenMM PDBFile
topology machinery recovers these bonds. The source graph did not previously
carry disulfide edges. The versioned extension retains both complete sidechains
and their S–S bond, each neutral, with actual source coordinates and CB–CA caps.

Require unique source S–S connectivity, each partner's two CB H/no S H, and
1.8–2.3Å source S–S distance. A complete neutral thiol instead requires two CB H
and one S H. Unpaired deprotonated Cys, ambiguous bonds, incomplete partners,
unsupported species or inconsistent protonation fail explicitly. No proton or
disulfide is invented. Installed topology-reader source is pinned in provenance.
